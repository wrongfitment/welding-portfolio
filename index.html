As a proud Albertan, I take great pride in the quality of work, craftsmanship, and education of both myself and my colleagues in the welding and manufacturing industry.

I am available full-time, including days, nights, and weekends. I bring a strong work ethic, extensive experience, and a passion for mentoring others in the trades.

I hold Red Seal, CWB, Safety, First Aid, Confined Space, Forklift, and Welding Inspector certifications, and have 5+ years of hands-on welding and manufacturing experience.

Equipped with my own tools for layout, fabrication, and finishing, I can handle both large structural projects and smaller detailed work.

Maintaining professionalism, reliability, and accuracy is my priority on every project.
