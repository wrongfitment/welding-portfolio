02 About

Proud Albertan.
Prouder of the welds.

I take real pride in craftsmanship — my own, and that of the professionals I've worked alongside in the welding and manufacturing industry. Quality work isn't a slogan to me; it's the standard every bead gets held to.

Frequently recognized for reliability, accuracy, and attention to detail through every stage of a project.

I hold a Red Seal Certification in Welding from SAIT and current CWB W47.1 qualifications in FCAW and SMAW, plus an ABSA Grade B Pressure Welder Certificate of Competency, with years of hands-on experience across structural steel, aluminum, and stainless. I'm fully equipped with my own tools for layout, fabrication, and finishing — from large structural builds to small detailed work — with reliable transportation to get wherever the job is.

Available full-time: days, nights, and weekends. I bring a strong work ethic, real industry experience, and a genuine drive to help others level up in the trades.
