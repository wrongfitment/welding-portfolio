About Me

As a proud Albertan, I take great pride in the quality of work, craftsmanship, and education of both myself as well as the professionals I’ve had the privilege of working alongside in the welding and manufacturing industry.

I am available to work full-time, including days, nights, and weekends. I bring a strong work ethic, extensive industry experience, and a genuine passion for helping others develop their skills in the trades.

I hold a Red Seal Certification in Welding from SAIT and have over five years of hands-on experience in welding and manufacturing. I am proficient in multiple welding processes and consistently deliver high-quality work with efficiency and precision.

I am fully equipped with my own layout, fabrication, and finishing tools for both large structural builds and detailed custom projects, and I have reliable transportation.

I maintain a professional and respectful demeanor in all workplace environments and am known for reliability, accuracy, and attention to detail.
