As a proud Albertan, I take great pride in the quality of work, craftsmanship, and education—both my own and that of the professionals I’ve had the privilege of working alongside in the welding and manufacturing industry.

I am available to work full-time, including days, nights, and weekends. I bring with me a strong work ethic, extensive industry experience, and a genuine passion for helping others develop their skills in the trades.

I hold a Red Seal Certification in Welding from SAIT and have hands-on experience in the welding and manufacturing sector. I have developed proficiency in a wide range of welding processes and consistently produce high-quality work with efficiency and precision.

I maintain a professional and respectful demeanor in all workplace interactions and am frequently recognized for my reliability, accuracy, and attention to detail through every stage of a project.
